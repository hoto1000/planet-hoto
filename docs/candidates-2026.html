2026 Senate and governor candidates

Sourced explainer · Checked October 22, 2026 · Election day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026

This page lists every Democratic and Republican nominee for US Senate and governor this year. It does not endorse anyone. States are in alphabetical order, and so are the two parties.

How this was checked: Each name comes from the race's Wikipedia article and was then matched against 270toWin's candidate data. Every Democratic and Republican name on this page appears in both. Independents are noted only when both sources list them as general election candidates. Libertarian and other third-party candidates are not listed. Candidates can withdraw late, so confirm with your sample ballot before you vote.

House and local races

All 435 House seats and thousands of state and local offices are also on the ballot. These free, nonpartisan tools show the exact candidates on your own ballot:

  • VOTE411, from the League of Women Voters. Enter your address for a personal voter guide with candidates' own answers.
  • Ballotpedia's sample ballot lookup. It covers federal, state, and many local races.
  • vote.gov, the official US government site, for registration and your state's election office.
  • Your county or state election office, which publishes the official sample ballot.

What you can do with this

Look up your own ballot, then read each candidate's own website and a news source you trust. If something on this page is wrong or out of date, report it with a link to an official source.
