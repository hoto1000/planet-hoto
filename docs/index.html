About

What we promise

  • Plain language. If a sentence needs a dictionary, we rewrite it.
  • Sources you can check. Every guide links to where its claims come from.
  • Global, locally aware. Mental health is universal, but systems and words are not. Guides start with principles that travel and point to local services. Translations are drafts and we are improving them with native speakers.
  • No diagnosing, no scoring. We explain and point you to people who can help. We do not replace them.
  • Your information stays yours. This site does not collect or store what you tell it.
  • Equity first. Care is easier to reach for some people than others, and differs a lot between countries. Our guides say so and plan around it.
