Where do I stand?

Questionnaire · US issues · Private, nothing is stored or sent

Most people agree with different parties on different issues. This short questionnaire shows where your answers lean on a few broad questions, so you can think about what matters to you. It is not voting advice, it does not tell you which party to pick, and it is rough: a handful of statements cannot capture your views.

How it works. Choose the topics you want, rate each statement from strongly disagree to strongly agree (or skip it), then see a summary. Your answers stay in this browser tab. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent anywhere. The statements are about US politics.

1. Pick your topics

    Your summary

    Each bar shows where your answers lean on one question, not a label for you. The middle means your answers were mixed or balanced. People who sit near each end often have thoughtful reasons, and many people sit near the middle on some topics and far from it on others. Compare with what supporters and critics say about each US party, and then look up your own candidates.

    How the statements were written

    Each topic has four statements: two where agreeing points one way and two where it points the other, so that agreeing with everything does not push the result to one side. Wording tries to avoid loaded terms. A neutral score of "neither agree nor disagree" counts as the middle, and statements you skip are left out. A topic with no answers is not shown.
